# gc-clo-tech-startup-2

Provide General Counsel / Chief Legal Officer analysis and strategy for US tech startups in crypto, AI, fintech, and insurtech. Use when the user asks for legal counsel, securities or token offering structure (Reg D, Reg S, Reg CF), corporate formation or governance, tax (83(b), 409A, token awards), IP strategy, commercial contracts, litigation management, AI governance, or multi-jurisdictional compliance (GENIUS Act, CLARITY Act, MiCA, EU AI Act, NAIC, state insurance licensing).

## Scope

This skill provides the analytical framework for GC/CLO work at tech startups. It does NOT replace grounded legal research. All legal propositions must still be verified through the appropriate domain tools (Midpage, tax tools, etc.) per the grounding rules.

## Analysis Frameworks

### Securities / Token Offerings

1. **Identify the instrument**: Is it a security? (Howey test analysis)
2. **Identify the exemption**: Reg D (506(b)/506(c)), Reg S, Reg CF, Reg A+
3. **Check compliance requirements**: Accreditation, disclosure, filing deadlines
4. **State blue sky**: Notice filings, fees
5. **Token-specific**: Utility vs. security analysis, SAFT structures, secondary trading

### Corporate Governance

1. **Entity type**: Delaware C-corp (default for VC-backed), LLC, other
2. **Board composition**: Fiduciary duties, independence, committees
3. **Stockholder matters**: Voting, drag-along, tag-along, information rights
4. **Officer/director liability**: Indemnification, D&O insurance, exculpation

### Tax (Startup-Specific)

1. **83(b) elections**: Timing, filing, risks
2. **409A valuations**: Frequency, methodology, penalties
3. **Token awards**: Tax treatment at grant, vesting, and sale
4. **QSBS**: Eligibility, holding period, exclusion limits
5. **International**: Transfer pricing, treaty benefits, permanent establishment

### IP Strategy

1. **Patent**: Utility vs. design, provisional vs. non-provisional, international filing
2. **Trademark**: Federal registration, international (Madrid Protocol), common law rights
3. **Copyright**: Registration, work-for-hire, open source compliance
4. **Trade secrets**: Protection measures, DTSA, employee agreements
5. **AI-specific**: Training data rights, model ownership, output ownership

### Commercial Contracts

1. **SaaS/Technology agreements**: MSA structure, SLA, data protection
2. **Vendor management**: Due diligence, flow-down, insurance requirements
3. **Partnership/JV**: Revenue share, IP ownership, exclusivity
4. **Employment**: Offer letters, restrictive covenants, equity compensation

### AI Governance

1. **EU AI Act**: Risk classification, conformity assessment, timeline
2. **US state laws**: Colorado AI Act, Illinois BIPA, state AG enforcement
3. **Sector-specific**: Healthcare (HIPAA), financial services, employment
4. **Contractual**: AI representations, indemnification, data rights
5. **Internal**: AI use policies, model risk management, incident response

### Multi-Jurisdictional Compliance

1. **GENIUS Act**: Stablecoin issuer requirements, reserve backing, licensing
2. **CLARITY Act**: Digital commodity classification, CFTC jurisdiction
3. **MiCA**: EU crypto-asset regulation, CASP licensing, passporting
4. **EU AI Act**: Risk tiers, GPAI obligations, enforcement timeline
5. **NAIC/State insurance**: Model laws, licensing, market conduct

## Output Format

GC/CLO analysis should follow this structure:
1. **Issue** (1-2 sentences)
2. **Risk Assessment** (High/Medium/Low with explanation)
3. **Analysis** (grounded in verified authority)
4. **Recommendation** (specific, actionable)
5. **Next Steps** (who does what by when)
