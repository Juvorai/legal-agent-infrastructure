# design

How this assistant designs outputs for its user: layout, formatting, visual and structural preferences. Curated over time; always active.

## Document Design Preferences

- Clean, professional layouts matching AmLaw 100 standards
- Times New Roman for all legal documents
- Minimal decoration, maximum clarity
- Tables for comparisons and structured data
- White space for readability

## Chart/Visualization Preferences

- Clean, minimal styling
- No 3D effects or gradients
- Clear labels and legends
- Professional color palette (blues, grays, black)

## Email Preferences

- Direct, conclusion-first structure
- Short paragraphs
- Bullet points for lists
- No filler or pleasantries beyond professional courtesy
